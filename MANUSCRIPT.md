Russian review draft: https://claude.ai/artifact/WGneRnP5XHoKCkStmLEj8t

All numbers quoted in the manuscript come from `docs/stats_report.md`, which is generated by
`scripts/10_stats.py`, `scripts/13_multivariable.py` and `scripts/14_active_site_sensitivity.py` from the files in `data/processed/`. Nothing in `data/interim/`,
`data/processed/` or `results/` is edited by hand.

## Open items carried into the manuscript as stated gaps

- ΔΔG is not computed. DynaMut2 is a web server; the submission list of 1724 unique protein
  changes is `results/ddg_input_dynamut2.csv`. Place the returned file at
  `data/raw/dynamut2_results.csv` and rerun `scripts/08_ddg.py`.
- ClinVar release date is not recorded in `data/raw/MANIFEST.jsonl`.
- Version, assay and acceptance thresholds of the Amicus amenability table are not stated by the
  manufacturer; the amenability criterion used here is reproduced from the table as published.
- Ortholog source release and download date are not recorded.
- Domain boundaries are deliberately absent from `config.yaml` pending a citable source.
- Identity-threshold sensitivity for the MSA (0.40 vs the 0.55 used) is not run.

The harmonised table holds 3505 unique coding-level changes (`docs/audit_report.md`), of which
1109 are ClinVar pathogenic or likely pathogenic. The figure 3533 in earlier drafts was the row
count of the superseded merged CSV in `data/legacy/` and is not used.

See `LOG.md` for the full provenance record and `docs/audit_report.md` for the harmonisation audit.
